# PrepSuite Logo Ideas — AI-Generated

Ideas for creating an AI-generated logo for PrepSuite, a chess scouting and opponent analysis platform.

## Brand Context

- **Product**: Chess opponent analysis and preparation tool
- **Keywords**: Scouting, preparation, analysis, openings, strategy, tournament
- **Current visual**: Shield icon, indigo/purple accent (#6366f1), dark theme
- **Tone**: Professional, analytical, trustworthy

---

## Logo Concept Directions

### 1. Shield + Chess Piece
*"A shield protecting or framing a stylized chess knight or king, minimal flat design, indigo and white on dark background, app icon style, 512x512"*

- Combines protection (preparation) with chess
- Aligns with existing shield in nav
- Works well at small sizes

### 2. Knight + Magnifying Glass / Target
*"Minimalist chess knight merged with a magnifying glass or crosshair target, scouting and analysis concept, indigo gradient, flat vector, square format"*

- Emphasizes "scouting" and "analysis"
- Knight = strategic piece, good symbol for preparation

### 3. Stylized Pawn Formation
*"Three abstract pawns in formation, suggesting preparation and strategy, geometric minimal design, indigo and slate, icon for chess app"*

- Pawns = preparation, building position
- Clean, scalable

### 4. Knight + Document / Report
*"Chess knight integrated with a document or report page, AI analysis concept, clean line art, indigo accent, dark mode friendly"*

- Ties chess to "report" / "scouting report"
- Good for a dashboard/product logo

### 5. "PS" Monogram
*"Stylized PS monogram with subtle chess motif (e.g. knight silhouette in negative space), indigo, modern sans-serif feel, square icon"*

- PrepSuite initials
- Professional, versatile

### 6. Chess Board + Scan Line
*"Minimal chess board corner with a horizontal scan or analysis line across it, tech + chess, indigo glow, flat design"*

- Suggests "scanning" or "analyzing" games
- Tech-forward

### 7. Rook as Dossier
*"Rook chess piece stylized as a folder or dossier, preparation and scouting concept, indigo, minimal 3D or flat"*

- Rook = solid, reliable
- Dossier = scouting report

### 8. Knight + Chart / Graph
*"Chess knight with a small chart or upward trend line, analytics and performance concept, indigo and white, app icon"*

- Connects chess to "rating" and "performance"
- Fits Player Activity / progress chart

Minimal flat icon, chess knight and shield combined, indigo #6366f1 and white, dark background, 512x512, no text, app icon style
```

```

---

## Tools to Try

- **DALL·E 3** (ChatGPT Plus): Good for clean, literal concepts
- **Midjourney**: Strong for stylized, artistic logos
- **Ideogram**: Good at text + icon combinations
- **Figma + AI plugins**: For iterating on a base concept
- **Recraft** (recraft.ai): Vector-style logos, good for icons

---

## Recommendations

1. **Start with Concept 1 (Shield + Knight)** — matches current brand, easy to recognize
2. **Test Concept 5 (PS monogram)** — versatile for favicon, social, and print
3. **Ensure dark-background legibility** — PrepSuite uses dark theme; logo should work on `#0f172a` (slate-950)
