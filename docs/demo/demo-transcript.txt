0s — Your next opponent. Meet your preparation advantage.
2s — Start with a name. Your signed-in workspace keeps scouting, reports and AI together.
5s — Connect the dots. Add known Chess.com or Lichess accounts to guide the search.
8s — Let the research begin. Resolve the player, discover accounts and collect available games.
11s — The games become a report. See the available sources. Coverage varies by player.
14s — Spot the patterns. Compare opening frequency and results by color and game source.
17s — Build your game plan. Choose your color and filters. Start with the most frequent replies.
20s — Ask a better question. Ask the AI about this opponent’s openings, lines or positions.

26s — Get an answer you can inspect. Use game counts and opening results to decide what to review.
30s — Follow the evidence. Replay the source games, move by move, on an interactive board.
33s — Turn preparation into practice. Rehearse recorded opening lines, then continue with an approximate engine.
36s — See the bigger picture. Review rating history and time-control context.
39s — Take the plan with you. Download a round sheet or export the full report as a PDF.
42s — Your research stays ready. Reopen saved reports from History before your next round.
45s — Prepare for the whole field. Set up reports for up to five players and organize them in a folder.
